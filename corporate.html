Atomic escrow and temporary lock engine

For Sub-Registrars, AC (Land) officers, banks and legal teams.

  1. Owner A signs the deed
  2. Sub-Registry Office generates a deed registration token
  3. Temporary escrow lock (1 second). The original owner cannot sell again, secondary buyers cannot buy, and banks cannot mortgage.
  4. Automatic digital notice goes to the AC (Land) Office
  5. Mutation is completed
  6. Ownership is permanently transferred

Real-time state machine

ACTIVE_VERIFIED

The plot is clean and tradeable.

TEMPORARY_ESCROW_LOCK

Starts the moment a deed registration token is generated at the Sub-Registry. The plot is frozen across the national database. Neither the seller nor any third party can start another deed, power of attorney or mortgage on it.

PERMANENT_RECORD_UPDATED

Starts when the AC (Land) office finishes e-Mutation. The lock is released and the buyer becomes the permanent titleholder.

Banks and legal teams can confirm a plot is not in a hidden transition window before they lend or buy.

    Topsoil theft and farmland protection. Free Earth-observation satellites scan the country every few days. Farmland is normally green and moist, so a sudden dry, barren patch means topsoil was scraped away or filled with clay or sand. The system flags the exact plot and alerts the Upazila Agriculture Officer and AC (Land).

    Balu Geofence

    Sand mining and riverbank defense. The government draws the legal Balu Mahal box on the river map, and every legal dredger carries a solar-powered GPS tracker. Inside the box is fine. Outside the line, or running the pump during banned night hours, sends an instant alert to the River Police and the Deputy Commissioner (DC).
